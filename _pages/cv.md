PhD Candidate at the Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL — thesis submission expected May 2027. My research develops *probiotic architecture*: a design approach that treats the indoor microbiome as a material to be cultivated rather than sanitised. Computational design, robotic clay printing, and environmental sensing are integrated in my research to produce probiotic ceramic surfaces — most recently the *Living Assembly Pavilion* at the London Design Biennale 2025 (Special Mention).

Research Interests
======
Probiotic design · Bio-receptive design · Digital design and fabrication · Environmental sensing · Indoor microbiome and human health · Additive manufacturing · Machine learning for architectural design

  - Thesis: *Probiotic Modelling — Developing Computational Design Interventions for the Indoor Microbiome*
  - Supervisors: Prof. Marjan Colletti, Dr. Sean Nair
  - Funded by UCL EPSRC DTP Research Studentship
  - Interdisciplinary research in collaboration with microbiologists at Eastman Dental Institute, UCL
- **Master in Architectural Design (M.Arch)**, B-Pro, the Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL, London, UK (Sep 2021 – Sep 2022), with Distinction
  - Awarded Silver Medal in B-Pro Show 2022
  - Design project *Bio-Healer.ai* explores nature-based strategies that embed non-human biodiversity into buildings through computational design and machine learning

Research Experience
======
- **Visiting Researcher — GREENIN Mini-Fellowship**, Global Centre for Clean Air Research (GCARE), University of Surrey (Apr – Jul 2026)
  - Host: Prof. Prashant Kumar
  - Developing a mobile microclimate mapping system that combines UWB-based spatial positioning with SHT45 environmental sensing to characterise indoor air quality fields at architectural scale.

- **Doctoral Researcher**, Becket Lab, the Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL (Sep 2023 – Present)
  - PIs: Dr. Richard Beckett, Dr. Sean Nair
  - Embedded in the EPSRC-funded project *Developing Probiotic Interventions to Reduce the Emergence and Persistence of Pathogens in Built Environments* (ENG-EPSRC EFRI ELiS), in collaboration with Prof. Jack Gilbert's team at UCSD.
  - Working at the intersection of architecture and microbiology to develop living materials and architectural interventions that reduce Multi-Drug Resistant Organisms (MDROs) in indoor environments through beneficial *Bacillus*-based surfaces.
  - Integrates CFD simulation, robotic clay 3D printing, and microbiological material design for indoor microbiome modulation.
  - Flagship project *Living Assembly Pavilion: Probiotic Intervention* — London Design Biennale 2025 (Special Mention).
  - Developed a dual-nozzle robotic clay extrusion system at UCL Here East for bio-paste ceramic printing.
